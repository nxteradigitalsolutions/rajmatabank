Cumulative & Recurring Deposits

RD Installment Rules

  • Minimum monthly installment: ₹500
  • Standing Instruction (SI) from Savings Account is mandatory
  • Late payment penalty: ₹1.50 per ₹100 per month

Minor Accounts & Maturity

  • Accounts for minors aged 10+ can be self-operated
  • Maturity proceeds credited only to linked Savings Account
  • Nomination facility is mandatory for all deposit accounts
