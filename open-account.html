Documentation & Eligibility Guide

Your journey to prosperity begins with the right paperwork. Explore our comprehensive guide for opening accounts and availing banking facilities.

person

1. Savings Account Requirements

Designed for individuals seeking safety, liquidity, and moderate growth for their personal wealth.

check_circle

Identity Proof (OVD)

Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, Passport, or Driving License.

check_circle

Address Proof

Utility Bills (not older than 2 months), Property Tax receipt, or Bank Statement.

check_circle

Photographs

2 Recent Passport size color photographs.

check_circle

PAN Card

Mandatory for all savings accounts (Form 60 if PAN is unavailable).

Note: For minor accounts, parent/guardian documents are required along with the minor's birth certificate.

business

2. Current Account Requirements

Business Identity

GST Registration Certificate, Trade License, or Shops & Establishment Act Certificate.

Entity Proof

Partnership Deed, MOA/AOA (for Companies), or Trust Deed as applicable.

Taxation

Entity PAN Card and PAN of all authorized signatories/directors.

key

3. Locker Facility Details

Agreement

Stamping and execution of the Locker Agreement on requisite non-judicial stamp paper.

Security Deposit

FDR equivalent to 3 years' rent plus breakage charges must be maintained.

KYC Update

Full KYC compliance as per latest RBI guidelines for all locker hirers.
